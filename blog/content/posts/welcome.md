FORGE stands for **Frontier Offensive Research & Generative Exploitation**,
part of Microsoft Security.

This blog provides a home for public research notes alongside the lab's
[public repository](https://github.com/microsoft/forge-lab).

## Public findings

The repository maintains a cumulative
[CVE dataset](https://github.com/microsoft/forge-lab/blob/main/cve/cve.json),
updated as findings are cleared for public disclosure.

## Responsible disclosure

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.
Follow the reporting guidance in the repository's
[security policy](https://github.com/microsoft/forge-lab/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
